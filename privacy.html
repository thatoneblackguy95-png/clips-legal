Clip$ Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Clip$ is a desktop application that runs locally on the owner's own computer. It helps the owner turn long-form videos into short clips and publish those clips to the owner's own connected platform accounts (such as YouTube and TikTok).

Information the application handles

  • Platform sign-in tokens. When the owner connects a platform account, the sign-in token issued by that platform is stored only on the owner's own computer, inside the application's local data folder. Tokens are never sent to us or to any third party other than the platform that issued them.
  • Video files and text. Videos, transcripts, titles, and descriptions are processed on the owner's own computer. Video files and metadata are sent to a platform (such as YouTube or TikTok) only when the owner directs the application to publish them there.
  • No accounts with us. Clip$ has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no server of ours that receives your data.

What we collect

We collect nothing. Clip$ has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no telemetry. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we never receive any.

Third-party platforms

When the owner publishes through Clip$, the receiving platform's own privacy policy governs the content once it is on that platform. Owners are responsible for reviewing the terms and policies of each platform they connect.

Data removal

Because all application data lives on the owner's own computer, the owner can remove it at any time by deleting the application's data folder and revoking the application's access from the connected platform's settings page.
