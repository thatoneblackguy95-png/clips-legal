Clip$

Turn long-form videos into short clips, published to your own channels at your direction.

What Clip$ does

Clip$ is a desktop application that runs locally on the owner's computer. It scans long-form videos, finds the strongest moments, cuts them into short-form clips, writes titles and descriptions, and publishes the finished clips to the owner's own connected accounts on platforms such as YouTube and TikTok.

How it works

  • Runs on the owner's own computer — no Clip$ cloud, no uploads to us.
  • Connects to platform accounts only through each platform's official sign-in.
  • Sign-in tokens stay on the owner's device and can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings.
  • Nothing is published unless the owner has approved it.
